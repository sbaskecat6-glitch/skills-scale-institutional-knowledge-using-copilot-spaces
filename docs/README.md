# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory is the central index for OctoAcme's project management process guidance. It gives teams a quick way to find the standards, templates, and review workflows used across projects.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project model that starts with clear problem framing and measurable outcomes. During initiation, teams confirm the business need, identify stakeholders, define success criteria, and decide whether an initiative is ready to move into planning. Planning then turns the approved initiative into a backlog, timeline, and release plan with explicit owner, acceptance criteria, and Definition of Done. During execution, the team works in small delivery increments, tracks progress on a project board, holds regular standups and demos, and uses CI, tests, and review gates to maintain quality.

The process also emphasizes proactive risk and communication management. Projects maintain a risk register, escalate blockers by severity, and communicate progress according to a regular cadence with stakeholders and team leads. When a feature is ready, release and deployment follow a defined checklist with smoke testing, security review, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication. After each milestone or release, the team captures learning in a retrospective and feeds improvements back into the process to make the next cycle smoother and more effective.
